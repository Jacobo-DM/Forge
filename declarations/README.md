# Declarations

This space holds statements of orientation, position, and refusal.

Declarations are not instructions, and they are not internal working notes.
They name a stance — something that shapes how the work is carried forward.

They sit between root notes and covenant:
- less structural than root notes
- less binding than covenant
- more explicit than either
