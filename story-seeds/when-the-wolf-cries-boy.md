# When the Wolf Cries, Boy

Once upon a time a father and son were walking in the dark of a forest after a long day’s journey.

They kept quiet, as they often did in such moments, where their thoughts were turned toward hearth and home.

And on one such evening, the silence was broken by the wild growling and barking of wolves.

They quickened their pace, away from where they thought they might be gathered.

The next day they returned and found the bones of a young wolf that had been eaten by the wolves the night before.

“How could they do such a thing,” the boy thought.

But he kept the question in the lump in his throat, just as his father did.

And as they made their way out of the woods, the boy looked back.

A young wolf emerged.

Their eyes met for a moment.

The boy understood.
